# Privacy Policy – RoCheck

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

RoCheck ("the Bot", "we", "our", or "us") is a Discord bot designed to help Roblox group communities check group membership information and determine when members may become eligible for group payouts.

This Privacy Policy explains what information RoCheck processes, how that information is used, how it may be stored, and what choices users have when interacting with the Bot.

By using RoCheck, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on how you interact with RoCheck and how the Bot is configured by the server administrator, we may process the following information:

### 2.1 Discord Information

- Discord user IDs, when needed to identify users interacting with the Bot.
- Discord server and channel IDs, to identify the configured monitoring channel.
- Message IDs and timestamps, when needed to track or process messages.
- Message content posted in the designated monitoring channel.

### 2.2 Roblox Information

When a Roblox username is submitted, RoCheck may process:

- Roblox usernames and corresponding user identifiers.
- Roblox group membership information.
- Group membership or join dates, when available.
- Calculated waiting periods and payout eligibility results.

### 2.3 Technical and Operational Information

The Bot may process limited technical information necessary to operate reliably, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service availability. This may include error logs, command activity, and processing timestamps.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by RoCheck is used only for purposes related to the Bot's functionality, including:

- Detecting and extracting Roblox usernames from messages in the designated Discord channel.
- Checking Roblox group membership information.
- Calculating the required 14-day membership waiting period.
- Informing users or server moderators about membership and payout eligibility.
- Recovering or processing relevant checks after service interruptions or restarts.
- Maintaining, securing, and troubleshooting the Bot.

We do not use message content for advertising, behavioral profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## 4. Message Content Processing

RoCheck uses Discord's Message Content Intent to automatically detect and process ordinary messages in a server-designated channel.

When a message is submitted in that channel, the Bot may read its content to identify a Roblox username and perform the requested membership check.

The monitoring functionality is limited to channels configured by the server administrator. RoCheck does not need to monitor every channel in a Discord server.

The Bot may review relevant channel history when necessary to recover missed checks after a restart or service interruption.

Message content is processed for the functionality described in this policy. Extracted usernames and related check results may be retained when necessary to provide the Bot's services.

**The Bot operator should ensure that the actual storage and logging configuration follows this policy.**

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases or infrastructure services used to operate RoCheck.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality, maintain operational reliability, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve service-related issues.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the Bot's operational requirements.

We do not intend to retain complete message histories for unrelated purposes.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

RoCheck may interact with third-party services required for its functionality, including:

- **Discord:** To receive messages and interactions and deliver Bot responses.
- **Roblox:** To retrieve publicly available Roblox account and group membership information.
- **Database and hosting providers:** To store necessary operational data and run the Bot.

Information may be transmitted to these services as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.

We do not sell users' personal information or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 7. AI and Machine Learning

RoCheck does not use message content or processed user information to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

## 8. User Choices and Data Requests

Users can choose not to submit Roblox usernames or other information to RoCheck.

Users who do not wish to have their messages processed by the Bot should avoid submitting messages in the designated monitoring channel and may contact the relevant server administrator about alternative participation arrangements.

For questions, access requests, or requests to delete information associated with their use of RoCheck, users may contact the Bot operator using the contact information provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where necessary for security, operational, or legal reasons.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by RoCheck against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children's Privacy

RoCheck is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to its functionality.

Users should follow Discord's and Roblox's applicable age requirements and terms of service when using the Bot.

We may update this Privacy Policy when RoCheck's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published at the same public URL. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
